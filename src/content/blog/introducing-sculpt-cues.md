Sculpt classes live and die on two things: a sequence that works the whole body without frying one muscle, and a teacher who stays locked onto the music.

Most of us plan in notes apps and count beats in our heads. Sculpt Cues brings both into one place.

## Plan the class

Build a **Flow** – your class – out of segments, one per song. Pick from 280+ movements sorted by body part, or add your own. As you build, Sculpt Cues suggests what could come next.

## Check the load

Every movement is scored across seven body parts. Open the Intensity chart and you'll see exactly where the work is going, with a caution when one area is doing too much and a counter movement to balance it.

## Cue the beat

Play any song out loud and Cue Coach finds the BPM live, then pulses Up and Down cues on the beat. Half time for slow presses, double time for pulses. The audio never leaves your phone.
