The structure follows our [45-minute sculpt class template](/blog/how-to-plan-a-sculpt-class/): warm up, build, peak and come back down.

<pre id="sc-class-plan" class="sc-copy-text" tabindex="0">SCULPT CLASS · 45 MIN · 14 SONGS (46:05)
Music: HIGH ENERGY SCULPT PILATES by Lizzie Schramm (Spotify)
Equipment: mat, light dumbbells, loop band (optional)

1. WARM-UP (0:00–3:03)
Song: Keeping Your Head Up (Jonas Blue Remix) – Birdy, Jonas Blue (3:03)
- March in place: tall spine, easy arm swing
- Squat to overhead reach: sit back, reach long
- Arm circles: small to big, shoulders down
- Hip hinge: soft knees, long flat back

2. STANDING LEGS: SQUATS (3:03–7:18)
Song: Latch – Disclosure, Sam Smith (4:15)
- Squat: weight in the heels, chest proud
- Sumo squat: knees track over toes
- Sumo pulse: stay low, small range on the beat
- Squat hold with heel lifts: hips stay still

3. STANDING LEGS: LUNGES (7:18–10:23)
Song: San Frandisco – Dom Dolla (3:05)
- Reverse lunge, right: step back, front knee stacked
- Reverse lunge, left: same count, same depth
- Lunge pulse: down an inch, up an inch
- Split-stance hold: hips square, breathe

4. UPPER BODY: PRESS AND ROW (10:23–13:54)
Song: Pick Up The Phone – SOFI TUKKER, Nonô (3:31)
- Overhead press: ribs down, press straight up
- Bent-over row: hinge, elbows brush the ribs
- Press and row combo: 8 of each, then 4, then 2
- Lateral raise: lead with the elbows, stop at shoulder height

5. UPPER BODY: ARMS (13:54–16:55)
Song: Rhyme Dust – MK, Dom Dolla (3:01)
- Bicep curl: elbows pinned to the sides
- Hammer curl: thumbs up, slow on the way down
- Half-range curls: bottom half, then top half
- Curl hold at 90 degrees: wrists long, shoulders soft

6. GLUTES: STANDING (16:55–19:50)
Song: More Baby – Chris Lake, Aluna (2:55)
- Curtsy lunge, right: step behind, hips face forward
- Curtsy lunge, left: same depth both sides
- Standing kickback: squeeze at the top, no arching
- Banded side steps: stay low, toes forward

7. GLUTES: MAT (19:50–23:29)
Song: Blessings – Calvin Harris, Clementine Douglas (3:39)
- Glute bridge: press through the heels
- Bridge pulse: lift an inch, lower an inch
- Single-leg bridge, right then left: hips level
- Bridge hold with knee opens: band above the knees

8. UPPER BODY: CHEST AND TRICEPS (23:29–26:38)
Song: Peace of Mind – Disco Lines, Ship Wrek, Daya (3:09)
- Chest press on the mat: elbows at 45 degrees
- Chest fly: soft elbows, hug a big tree
- Tricep kickback: upper arm still, extend fully
- Push-up on knees: one line from knees to head

9. CORE 1 (26:38–29:35)
Song: Afraid To Feel – LF SYSTEM (2:57)
- Dead bug: low back stays heavy on the mat
- Crunch with reach: chin off the chest
- Toe taps: exhale as the foot lowers
- Hollow hold: arms by the ears or by the hips

10. CORE 2 (29:35–32:00)
Song: Restless Bones – Disco Lines (2:25)
- Plank shoulder taps: hips stay still
- Side plank hip dips, right: lift from the waist
- Side plank hip dips, left: same count
- Bicycle: slow, rotate from the ribs

11. BURNOUT: LEGS (32:00–34:33)
Song: Lost – RSCL, it's murph, Twin Diplomacy, Jack August (2:33)
- Squat pulse: stay low, single time
- Squat to calf raise: drive up, lower with control
- Wide squat hold: arms reach forward
- Squat pulse in double time: last 8 counts

12. FINISHER 1: FULL BODY (34:33–38:13)
Song: Go Back – John Summit, Sub Focus, Julia Church (3:40)
- Squat to press: stand tall, press on the way up
- Reverse lunge to curl: curl as you rise
- Plank to down dog: reach the hips back
- Pulses on the drop: shorter range, same tempo

13. FINISHER 2: PULSES AND HOLDS (38:13–42:06)
Song: The Days (NOTION Remix) – Chrystal, NOTION (3:53)
- Lunge pulse with bicep curl: right side
- Lunge pulse with bicep curl: left side
- Plank hold: long line, breathe
- Squat hold with arm pulses: finish strong

14. COOL-DOWN (42:06–46:05)
Song: You Were Right – RÜFÜS DU SOL (3:59, count in half time)
- Forward fold: soft knees, heavy head
- Standing quad stretch: knees together
- Figure-four on the mat: flex the top foot
- Chest opener and child's pose: slow breath out

TOTAL: 46:05 (fade the last track at 45:00 for a 45-minute class)</pre>

## How to teach to this sculpt class playlist

Most of the playlist sits between 122 and 132 BPM, which is a comfortable single-time count for squats, lunges and presses. A few notes from planning it:

- **Warm-up and Latch share a tempo (122 BPM).** That lets the room settle into one count before the lunges speed things up a little.
- **Core sits on the mat at 128 and 122 BPM.** Teach the dead bugs and hollow holds in half time, so every rep is slow and controlled. More on that in [matching sculpt movements to BPM](/blog/matching-sculpt-movements-to-bpm/).
- **The finisher climbs to 136 and 138 BPM.** Keep the Movements familiar. This is no time to teach something new.
- **The cool-down is not slow on paper.** You Were Right lists at 122 BPM, so count it in half time (one move every two beats) and let the stretches breathe.

For more on choosing tempos per segment, see our guide to [sculpt class music and BPM](/blog/sculpt-class-music-bpm/).

## Make the playlist your own

Swap any song for one you love, as long as it's a similar length and tempo. Keep the segment order and the timings still work. A few easy changes:

- **Short on time?** Drop segments 11 and 13 for a 40-minute class.
- **Running 60 minutes?** Add a second upper-body segment and a longer cool-down.
